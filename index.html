04 / RESEARCH

SENIOR HIGH SCHOOL RESEARCH

Level of Satisfaction towards Teleconsultation among Pototanons

This quantitative study examined how satisfied Pototan residents were with teleconsultation and whether satisfaction differed by sex and socioeconomic status.

The research involved 30 participants who had used teleconsultation for more than one year. Data came from a researcher-made questionnaire and were analyzed using SPSS, the Mann–Whitney U test, and the Kruskal–Wallis H test. Results showed generally positive satisfaction, with no significant difference based on sex or socioeconomic status.
